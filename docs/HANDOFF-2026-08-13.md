## 1. Committed and verified

Two rendering bugs, both reproduced on a real GPU (RTX 4060 / ANGLE D3D11) with a
before/after gate, both fixing what the owner reported as "flicker".

### `b3a6690` — AO flicker: a resize left `aoFB2` bound to a deleted depth texture

`aoResolve()` ends every frame by attaching `aoDepth` to `aoFB2`. `aoAlloc()` then
deletes and recreates `aoDepth` on any drawing-buffer size change, but reattached
**only** `COLOR_ATTACHMENT0` — leaving `aoFB2`'s `DEPTH_ATTACHMENT` pointing at the
just-deleted texture, which the attachment keeps alive at the OLD height.
`checkFramebufferStatus` → `INCOMPLETE_DIMENSIONS` → `aoReady=false` → the scene
skips AO, FXAA and bloom.

It also **latched**: `aoW=w; aoH=h;` was committed *before* the allocation could
fail, so the `if(aoW===w&&aoH===h) return;` guard short-circuited every retry. One
bad resize disabled the post chain for the rest of the session.

| | reallocations | incomplete | frames without post chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 7 | 4 | 875 / 1500 |
| after | 7 | 0 | 0 / 1322 |

Gate: `node tools/verify-ao-resize.mjs <serve-root> <port> [label]`

### `268f84c` — FX strobe: `perfScale` is a quality gate, so it must not be eased

~70 effects gate on `perfScale` with thresholds clustered in **0.30–0.56** (water
sparkle, shadow decals, tread dust, debris, rubble, repair beams, and AO at 0.50).
`perfBand` is quantised to {0.25, 0.55, 1} and already hysteretic — that is the
anti-strobe mechanism. But `perfScale` was *eased* toward it at 0.45/sample, and
the eased value is what every gate reads, so one band change dragged it through
the entire cluster and effects peeled off one at a time.

```
fps:       60  60  38  38  24  24  24  38  38  60  60  60  60
before   1.00 1.00 0.80 0.69 0.49 0.38 0.32 0.42 0.48 0.71 0.84 0.91 0.95   12 levels, 6 staggered events
after    1.00 1.00 0.55 0.55 0.25 0.25 0.25 0.55 0.55 1.00 1.00 1.00 1.00    3 levels, 2 clean steps
```

Second defect in the same block: `perfScale *= GFX.particles` ran after the ease so
its own output was re-eased and re-multiplied — it **compounded**. Measured on the
real GPU at **3.63–3.86 on cinematic** (intended ceiling 1.0, ~4× the particles
asked for) and **0.31 on low at a locked 60fps** (silently disabling every effect
gated above 0.32). Density now multiplies a value recomputed from `perfBand` each
sample instead of an accumulator.

Gate: `node tools/verify-perfscale-gates.mjs <serve-root> <port> [preset]` — it
measures the **spread** of gate crossings, not the count. Counting was the wrong
metric: a genuine band drop *should* cross every threshold below it, and that is
one deliberate quality step. What makes it a strobe is crossing them at different
times.

### Track A (icons) — reported result

Deviates from the brief with a stated reason: bake is 1024/16×16/64px composited
into the **right half of a 2048×1024** GPU texture whose left half is the existing
procedural 1024/8×8/128 canvas. One texture, so `render3d.js:2371` still draws the
whole map in **one** `bbIcon` call. Argument: icons draw at 15–24 CSS px, so even at
DPR3 the 128px mip is never sampled and a 2048² atlas costs 16 MB for nothing.

Two honest failures it reported, both worth keeping:
- **First bake overflowed.** `INNER=54` fitted glyphs to 84% of the cell against the
  placeholders' ~75%, so silhouettes spilled past the plates. Corrected to
  `INNER=46`. *Every count and gate passed at 54* — caught only by looking.
- **The brief's suggested capture span was wrong.** At `orthoSpan` 2200 **no**
  building iconises: `mfBldSpan` is footprint-based (`size*2.2`), so the smallest
  structure only begins fading in around span 1840+.

### Track B (context leak) — reported result

Chose the shared-context route over `loseContext()`+cap, on the grounds that a cap
picks an arbitrary number and everything past it stops rendering. One lazily-created
context (`mfIntel3DShared()`) serves every preview; card canvases became ordinary 2D
surfaces receiving a blit. Key fact: VAOs and buffers are per-**context**, not
per-canvas, so geometry stays resident and nothing is rebuilt per frame.

Measured: before, battlefield context lost after **24** cards (GRAPHICS PAUSED,
black ground, filmed). After, **72** cards (4.5× budget), context count constant at
3, zero warnings, ground luminance flat.

## 4. Traps that cost real time — do not rediscover

- **A fixed-viewport harness cannot see the AO bug.** Three runs totalling 4,400+
  frames showed `aoActive` on for every frame, zero toggles, one distinct
  `aoW/aoH`. The trigger is the resize itself.
- **Pixel diffing cannot judge this game.** Two runs of the *same* build differ by
  72% of pixels. Assert on state you can read (`gl.getUniform`, framebuffer status),

  live inside the repo, next to `node_modules`.

  forward-slash ROOT rejects every request and serves nothing but 404s. Normalise
  with `resolve()` on both sides.

  Refuse if `UNMASKED_RENDERER_WEBGL` matches `/swiftshader|software/`.

## 5. Wrong diagnoses from this session, recorded so they are not repeated

Each of these was disproved by checking, not by argument:

- **`beginBB` leaving a stale texture bind** — it binds `tex||atlasTex`, so it always
  rebinds.
- **`gpfxFrame` leaving a null VAO** — it does, but both `InstMesh.flush` and
  `BBBatch.flush` bind their own VAO on entry.
- **Depth-clear ordering** — correct; `aoBeginScene` at 614, clear at 624.
- **`uWorldPerZ=15000` making the AO bias sub-LSB** — **wrong, and nearly shipped as
  a fix.** The camera is orthographic (`m4ortho(matP,-hw,hw,-hh,hh,-6000,9000)`), so
  depth is linear: 24-bit over 15,000 world units is 0.00089/LSB, making the 0.35
  bias ~392 LSBs. Changing it would have altered AO across the whole game for
  nothing.
- **"Contrast collapse"** — inferred from a ratio between two frames that also
  differed by scene motion. Not a safe read.
- **`mfIconEnsure` latching the procedural atlas forever** — refuted at runtime;
  `tacticons.js:413` does invalidate `mfIcoTex` on decode.
