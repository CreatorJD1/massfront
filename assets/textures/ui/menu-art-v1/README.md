# MASSFRONT menu art v1

These sixteen panorama masters were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool
on 2026-09-12 for this project, then converted to 1536 px-wide WebP runtime
derivatives. The source compositions used MASSFRONT's NEXUS-VII blueprint and
Terran command cinematic only as visual-language references. The final pass was
explicitly restyled into clean, simplified, non-photorealistic RTS concept art.
The final correction seals every scene inside NEXUS-VII: there are no windows
or apertures exposing stars, planets, exterior ships, or an outdoor skyline.
Planet and fleet forms in Operations, Research, and Intel are interior
holographic projections, not exterior views.

- `operations-war-table-v1.webp`: Galactic operations and War Room.
- `arsenal-fabrication-bay-v1.webp`: Arsenal, construction, and inventory.
- `research-directorate-v1.webp`: Research and systems configuration.
- `contracts-intel-table-v1.webp`: Contracts, intel, and transmissions.
- `career-service-record-v1.webp`: uniform, medals, rank, and career record.
- `settings-calibration-bay-v1.webp`: audio, display, and input calibration hardware.
- `inbox-communications-v1.webp`: encrypted radio racks and transmission routing.
- `social-crew-lounge-v1.webp`: crew ready room, roster wall, and squad briefing space.
- `uga-navigation-bridge-v1.webp`: sealed bridge, helm consoles, and route table.
- `uga-survey-lab-v1.webp`: planetary samples, xenobiology, and survey instruments.
- `uga-habitat-medical-v1.webp`: bunks, communal space, and medical treatment bay.
- `uga-strike-hangar-v1.webp`: closed pressure doors, dropship clamps, and maintenance gantries.
- `uga-logistics-cargo-v1.webp`: cargo lanes, scanning arches, fuel, and secure stores.
- `uga-coalition-embassy-v1.webp`: faction negotiation stations and diplomatic chamber.
- `uga-command-concourse-v1.webp`: internal department junction and ship-services console.
- `uga-engineering-systems-v1.webp`: reactor conduits, turbines, and systems diagnostics.

The four later destinations came from a single 2-by-2 generation atlas and were
cropped into runtime panoramas. Its final prompt explicitly prohibited planets,
orbs, generic holograms, exterior views, readable copy, and repeated scene
motifs; each quadrant instead had to depict the physical work of its named room.

Production constraints: original scene composition, no readable text, logos,
emblems, watermarks, or copied franchise designs. Source PNG masters remain in
the Codex generated-image archive for this task; these WebP files are the
canonical runtime derivatives.

The eight UGA-specific masters are retained in the task archive as the
`exec-ebd8cbca`, `exec-fe181931`, `exec-92dd669a`, `exec-2083b777`,
`exec-7e15938e`, `exec-0552704a`, `exec-1d9b063f`, and `exec-6b6e230c`
generations. Each prompt explicitly required an armored sealed interior and
forbade windows, space, stars, planets, readable text, people, and generic orbs.
