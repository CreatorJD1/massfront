## Design contract

- Meridian is a believable civilian/industrial colony before it becomes an infestation site.
- Personnel, small vehicles, light mechs, and medium mechs share measured primary routes; personnel also receive shortcuts.
- Clean, breached, infested, and purged/ruined states remain deterministic and visually distinct.
- Colony paving, civic facades, service metal, glazing, road markings, rubble/scorch, chitin, vascular tissue, acid film, spore crust, and transition blends remain separate PBR families.
- Brood is non-playable, non-humanoid, and never represented as a civilian faction or ownership skin.
